📊 Day 4 – Data Visualization & Storytelling
Global Superstore Sales Story – Power BI
This project was completed as part of the Day 4 – Data Visualization and Storytelling internship task.

The objective is to transform structured sales data into a clear visual story that communicates trends, patterns, business insights, and recommendations.
🎯 Central Business Question
What is driving sales performance, and where should the business focus?

All visuals in this project are organized around this question.
🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI – Data analysis and visualization
- Microsoft PowerPoint – Visual storytelling and presentation
- Superstore Dataset – Sales analysis dataset
📌 Project Deliverables
The visual story contains the following sections:
1. Overall Sales Performance
Key performance indicators are used to establish the overall business situation.
- Total Sales: 12.64M
- Total Profit: 1.47M
- Customer Count: 1,590
- Order Count: 51.29K
Takeaway: Overall sales are strong, creating a baseline for deeper performance analysis.
2. Monthly Sales Trend
A monthly sales visualization is used to understand how sales change throughout the year.

Takeaway: Sales fluctuate across the year, with November and December among the stronger sales periods.
3. Regional Performance
Regional sales performance is analyzed to identify geographic differences.

Takeaway: Sales performance varies significantly between regions, highlighting areas with stronger contribution and potential growth opportunities.
4. Customer Segment Analysis
Sales are compared across:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.

Takeaway: Consumer is the strongest customer segment and should remain an important focus for the business.
5. Sales and Profitability
Sales performance is considered together with profit to avoid focusing only on revenue.

Takeaway: High sales do not necessarily mean equally high profitability, so both metrics should be considered when making business decisions.
6. Business Recommendations
Based on the visual analysis:
- Use strong sales periods for better inventory and campaign planning.
- Prioritize high-performing regions while investigating growth opportunities.
- Continue focusing on the Consumer segment because it is the largest sales contributor.
- Track profit along with sales to support profitable growth.
